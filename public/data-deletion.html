How to Delete Your Data via Facebook

According to Facebook Platform Rules and GDPR/CCPA regulations, you can revoke access and delete all data linked between your Facebook account and NXLY Stream at any time by following these official steps:

1
Open Facebook Settings

Go to your Facebook profile, tap your profile icon at top right, and select Settings & Privacy > Settings.

2
Navigate to Apps and Websites

In the left sidebar (or under Permissions on mobile), select Apps and Websites to view all connected apps.

3
Locate NXLY Stream

Scroll through your active applications and find NXLY (or NXLY Stream).

4
Click "Remove" and Confirm Deletion

Click the Remove button. In the pop-up modal, check the box: "Delete posts, videos or events NXLY posted on your timeline", then confirm by clicking Remove.

Once removed, Facebook notifies our server via our automated webhook callback, and any cached identifiers or tokens are permanently deleted from our system within 24 hours.

Check Deletion Status or Request Removal

If you have a confirmation code or want to submit an explicit data deletion request with your Facebook User ID or email, enter it below:

If you prefer to submit a manual deletion request or have questions regarding data privacy, our team is available 24/7:
