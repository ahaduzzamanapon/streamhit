About NXLY Stream

Empowering movie lovers with lightning-fast media discovery, multilingual cataloging, and intelligent playback solutions.

Our Mission

Founded in 2026, NXLY Stream (formerly known as Streamfit) was built with a singular vision: to create a seamless, high-performance entertainment discovery platform where viewers can explore global movies, trending TV series, and anime with zero clutter.

We believe that finding quality entertainment shouldn't mean navigating slow, broken interfaces. That’s why we engineered an ultra-fast web catalog paired with a dedicated, hardware-accelerated Android & Google TV application designed for cinematic quality.
